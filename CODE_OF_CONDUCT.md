# Code of conduct

Kairos is built in public by people with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. To keep it a place where good work happens:

- **Be respectful.** Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are honest; help fix them.
- **Be welcoming to newcomers.** Every expert once asked the obvious question.
- **No harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks**, in any
  project space: issues, pull requests, discussions or chat.
- **No promotion of scams or price speculation.** Kairos testnet coins have no
  value, and nobody may sell or market them as if they did.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may block people who repeatedly or
seriously break these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the
maintainers through the contact details on the repository owner's profile.
